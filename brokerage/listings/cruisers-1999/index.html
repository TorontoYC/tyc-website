Overview

The Cruisers 3750 is an aft cabin motor yacht. The salon, galley and dinette sit in the middle of the boat, a few steps down from the aft deck, with a stateroom at each end: a double berth forward and an island berth in the master aft. There are two heads, so two couples can share a long weekend aboard without lining up in the morning.

Topside there is an open aft deck under the hardtop, and a flybridge with seating across from the helm. Molded steps lead up from the swim platform, so boarding does not mean climbing a ladder.

The trade-off in this layout is space inside the cabins themselves. Both staterooms are sized for sleeping, and hanging locker space is limited.
