2002 Carver 410 Sport Sedan

Krates Marina, Keswick, Lake Simcoe

$265,000 CAD

Both Engines Rebuilt in 2026

Both engines on this boat were completely rebuilt in 2026 by an independent machine shop. Full overhaul: new bearings, pistons, connecting rods, camshaft, timing set, gaskets, fuel pump, exhaust manifolds and risers, then dyno tested before reinstall. The work orders and paid invoices come with the boat.

That is the reason to look at this one. Boats of this age and size almost always sell on original engines with well over a thousand hours on them. This one does not, and the paperwork is there to show it.

Mechanical condition is confirmed by your own surveyor and a mechanic of your choosing.

Two Staterooms, Two Heads

Forward master with a walkaround berth, hull portlights either side, an overhead hatch and cherry joinery throughout. Drawers under the berth and hanging lockers to both sides. Second cabin aft of the galley.

Two separate heads. One has an enclosed tub and shower, the second is a day head with a stainless sink and an opening portlight.

The salon seats seven or eight on a leather sectional with a flat screen forward. The galley is down and forward with a hardwood sole, full height fridge and freezer, cooktop, microwave and solid surface counters. A second lounge and table sit up on the flybridge under the hardtop.

Flybridge, Cockpit and Deck

Flybridge with a hardtop and a full camper back canvas enclosure in good order. The helm is up here, with a wet bar and sink, a lounge and cocktail table, and synthetic teak underfoot. Radar and satellite domes on the arch.

She carries a bow thruster, which on a 46 foot boat is the difference between a relaxed docking and a stressful one, especially single handed or crosswind at the slip.

The aft deck and swim platform are finished in synthetic teak decking with a transom door through to the platform. Foredeck carries two sunpads and a deck hatch.

Condition Details

The rebuild above is a documented work history, which is a different thing from us telling you the engines are sound. Your surveyor and your mechanic settle that, and we will help you book both.
