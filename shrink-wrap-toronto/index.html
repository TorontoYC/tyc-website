Boat Shrink Wrapping in Toronto and the GTA

We do not have a shop in the city, so Toronto boats get wrapped where they sit. Our installers bring the frame stock, the film and the heat, and the boat is covered before the first freeze. Framed, vented, heat sealed, and still on in March.

How mobile shrink wrapping works in the GTA

Tell us the length of the boat, where it is sitting and when it comes out of the water. We book a window, show up with everything on the truck, and wrap on site. Marina yards, storage lots, driveways and cottage properties all work. The boat can be on a trailer, on a cradle or on stands. You do not need to be there.

What you get

  • The frame goes up first. Film pulled straight over the rails lets water pool, and pooled water is what splits a cover open in February.
  • Four vents, so air moves through the cover. A sealed boat sweats, and that is where the mould comes from.
  • Seams heat sealed, straps run under the hull and pulled tight, so the cover is still on in March.
  • Four moisture absorbers included. Zipper access door, extra vents and extra absorbers available if you want them.

Where we wrap around Toronto

We cover the city and the GTA, and we work north from there to our three marina shops on Lake Simcoe and Georgian Bay. If your boat is being hauled and trailered north for the winter anyway, wrapping it at one of our shops is often simpler. Tell us the plan on the form and we will tell you which is cheaper.

When to book

Ontario haul out runs from early October through the end of November, and the yards back up in the middle of it. The earlier we know your haul out date, the better the window we can give you. Wrapping a boat in a January thaw is possible and it is nobody's first choice.

Questions

Do you shrink wrap boats in downtown Toronto?

Yes. We wrap wherever the boat is sitting, including marinas on the waterfront, storage lots and private driveways across the GTA.

How much does it cost?

We quote by the foot. Length is the main factor, then boat type and any add ons like a zipper door or extra vents. Mobile jobs in the city are quoted the same way. Send us the details and we will price it.

Can you wrap a boat that is still in the water?

No. The boat has to be hauled and sitting on a trailer, a cradle or stands before we can frame and wrap it.

Do you take the wrap off in the spring?

Yes. We remove the wrap and prep the boat for launch. Ask for it when you book and we will schedule both.
