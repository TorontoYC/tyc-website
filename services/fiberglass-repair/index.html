What We Do

Repair Work, Done Properly

Scratches & Gouges

Dock rash, prop strikes, and scratches deep enough to go through the gelcoat. Ground back to sound material, filled, faired, and sanded flat before any colour goes on.

Cracks & Impact Damage

Stress cracks, spider cracking around hardware, and damage from impact. The crack gets opened up and repaired from the inside out. Filling over a crack without opening it means it comes back.

Below the Waterline

Blistering, keel and skeg damage, and grounding damage. The boat comes out of the water and the laminate gets dried before anything is applied to it.

Gelcoat Colour Matching

Matched to the hull as it is now, not to the factory colour, then wet sanded, compounded, and polished so the repair blends into the surrounding finish.

What to Expect

Honest About the Finish

A good repair is invisible from a few feet away in normal light. Up close, under direct sun, on a dark or faded hull, you may still find it. Anyone who promises you will never see it again has not done many of them. What we will tell you before we start is which of those two your boat is, and what it would take to get closer.

Common Questions

FAQ

Can you match my hull colour?

Usually. White and off-white match well. Dark colours, metallics, and heavily faded hulls are harder, because we are matching the hull as it looks today rather than what it left the factory as. We will tell you what to expect before we start.

Do I have to haul the boat out?

For anything below the waterline, yes, and the laminate needs time to dry before repair. Topside and above-waterline work can often happen where the boat sits.

How long does a repair take?

It depends on depth and cure times more than on size. A single gouge can be a day. Blister work runs longer because of drying time. We give you a real timeline when we quote.

Will the repair be completely invisible?

See the finish section above. From normal viewing distance, yes. Under direct sun at close range on a dark hull, sometimes not.

Can you provide something for my insurance claim?

Yes. We can supply photos of the damage and a written estimate for your insurer.
