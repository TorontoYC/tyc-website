SeaDek Marine Flooring

Get an Estimate Request a Quote

SeaDek is closed-cell foam flooring made for boats. It grips when it's wet and it's easier on your feet than bare fiberglass, and it doesn't absorb water. We install genuine SeaDek on swim platforms, steps, cockpit floors and anywhere else people stand, cut to the shape of your boat.

Where it goes

  • Swim platforms and boarding steps, where people come aboard wet.
  • Cockpit floors, in place of worn non-skid or old snap-in carpet.
  • Helm stations and step pads, for standing at the wheel.
  • Gunwales, bow areas and hatch lids.

How we install it

Every installation starts with a template of the area on your boat, with the cutouts for hatches, cleats, drains and fittings marked. The pads are cut to that template, so they fit your boat instead of a generic shape. SeaDek is held down by its adhesive backing, and it only holds as well as the surface under it, so we clean and prep the surface before anything goes down. Then the pads are fitted and the edges and seams are checked.

SeaDek comes in a range of colours and textures, including teak-style lines like the ones in the photos on this page. We'll go through the options with you when we quote.

If the fiberglass underneath has cracks, soft spots or other damage, we fix that first. Flooring over it hides the problem and it keeps getting worse. Fiberglass and gelcoat repair

Common Questions

FAQ

Is it real SeaDek?

Yes. We install genuine SeaDek.

Does the boat have to come out of the water?

No. We can install it with the boat in the slip, on a trailer or on a cradle.

Can SeaDek go over my old non-skid?

In most cases, yes. The surface has to be clean and sound for the adhesive to hold, and we check that before we quote.

Can I choose the colour and pattern?

Yes. SeaDek comes in a range of colours and textures, including teak-style lines. We'll go through the options with you when we quote.
