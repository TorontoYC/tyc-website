Boat Shrink Wrapping

Shrink wrap is the difference between a boat that comes out of winter dry and one that comes out with mould in the cushions. We build a frame, wrap the boat tight, vent it so it breathes, and finish it so the cover is still on in March.

Request a Quote Call 289-325-0457

What you get

  • A built frame, not film stretched over the rails. The frame is what keeps water from pooling and tearing the cover open in February.
  • Vents, so the boat breathes. Trapped moisture is what causes mould and mildew.
  • Heat sealed seams and tight tie downs.
  • A zipper access door on request, if you want to get aboard over the winter.

Where we wrap

We wrap at all three of our shops: Krates Marina in Keswick, Driftwood Cove in Port Severn, and Grand Harbour in Brechin. We also come to you across Lake Simcoe, Georgian Bay and the GTA. On a trailer, on a cradle, or on stands.

Booking

We take a limited number of shrink wrap bookings each season, so every boat gets wrapped before the weather turns.

Questions

How much does it cost?

We quote by the foot. Length is the main factor, then boat type and any add ons like a zipper door or extra vents. Send us the details and we will price it.

When should I book?

Early. Spots fill, and marina haul out schedules back up through November. The sooner we know when your boat is coming out, the better we can slot you in.

Do I need to be there when you wrap it?

No. Tell us where the boat is and how it is sitting, and we handle it.

Can I get into the boat over the winter?

Yes, if you ask for a zipper access door when you book. Retrofitting one later is harder than putting it in the first time.
